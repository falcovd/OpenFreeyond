# Problem statement

> I am an adult who enjoys cozy games and screen-based hobbies, trying to spend relaxed free time in a personal, homely place that feels like mine and has someone living in it to connect with, but I find only generic games, chatbots or voice assistants that have no world, no personality and no sense of presence, because they are built for tasks or for generic play, not for a personal, story-like companionship. This makes me feel a little lonely and unengaged, and that something I would enjoy is missing.

## Impact
- Frequency: unknown (hypothesis: a few relaxed sessions per week).
- Severity: low to moderate; a "nice to have", not a pain with a cost.
- Users: unknown. Reachable group: friends and colleagues of the builder. Size beyond that is unknown.

## Assumptions to test
- **A1:** People in the persona group like relaxed, story-like, cozy browser experiences and spend free time on them.
- **A2 (riskiest):** They feel something is missing: existing games, chatbots and voice assistants do not give them a personal place with a character that feels present.
- **A3:** The impediment is lack of personalization and presence (a world and persona that is "theirs"), not price, access or time.
- **A4:** The feeling is mild loneliness or lack of engagement rather than indifference.
- **A5:** Talking by voice to a fictional character is appealing, not awkward.

## Falsification criteria (fixed after approval)
- **A1** is contradicted if fewer than 2 of 3 interviewees describe spending regular free time (weekly) on cozy, story-like or exploration games or similar experiences.
- **A2** is contradicted if most interviewees say they are satisfied with existing options and name no missing element.
- **A3** is contradicted if most interviewees name other impediments (price, time, access) and none mentions personalization or presence.
- **A4** is contradicted if most interviewees describe no frustration, loneliness or sense of missing something.
- **A5** is contradicted if most interviewees describe talking by voice to a fictional character as awkward or unappealing, or say they would not try it.
- Overall: refuted if A2 is contradicted.
