# Neighbourhood: verification checklist

Spec: `specs/neighbourhood/spec.md`. Plan: `docs/plans/2026-11-09-neighbourhood.md`. The builder fills this in (Task 14). Answer each line yes or no. The spec text is quoted as written. Only the builder judges these items.

Rows marked "Auto" are also covered by a unit test (file and test named in the row). The test proves the data and logic. A person still judges how it looks and feels, and the rows without "Auto" are judged by a person only.

NFR-6 is a stated limitation, not a deliverable: no accessibility level is promised, and the place works with keyboard and mouse or trackpad only, on desktop browsers (spec A-6).

## How to measure

Task 0 (before views, done on `main` before any change): `npm run build && npx vite preview --port 5188`, open `http://localhost:5188/?stats` in Chrome, choose Try-out. Save screenshots to `docs/verification/neighbourhood-before/` at fixed views: the spawn looking at the house; the middle of the garden looking at the house; the middle looking toward the street; near the bench looking at the character. Note the time of day (the sky drifts slowly: take them within a minute, or note the sky azimuth). Run the 2-minute frame-rate walk once and write the 12 values in the test note as the baseline.

The steps of Task 14 of the plan:

1. Walk and look. `npm run build && npx vite preview --port 5188`, Chrome, Try-out. Walk all four edges of our garden (our fence stops you each time), click the gates, door, cars and lamps of the neighbourhood (nothing happens), look at the plots, the houses (style test per house: tiled pitched or stepped-gable roof, brick or rendered facade, at least 4 windows facing the street, a front door on the street side), the cars (3 or more colours, facing the driving direction of their side), the lamps, and the backdrop all round; open the front door and watch the character for 5 minutes; go within 3 m of him (he stops, faces you, waves once).
2. Frame rate (NFR-1, AC-6.1). Open `http://localhost:5188/?stats`. Walk the 2 minutes (spawn, path, front door, every room, back out), writing the displayed value every 10 seconds (12 values) and the average; the walk starts as soon as the place is ready (D-35). Name the laptop in the test note.
3. If the average is below 30 (AC-6.2 to AC-6.4). Take the steps one after the other, measuring again after each: step 1 (`NEIGHBOUR_GARDENS` and `NEIGHBOUR_CARS` to `'off'`), step 2 (`NEIGHBOUR_SHADOWS` to `'off'`), step 3 (`FAR_PLOT_COUNT` to 3). Stop at the first measurement that meets 30 fps. Write each step in the checklist; with step 3 look at the street: each removed far plot is gone with its fence, gate, path, house, garden, car and lamp and its space is grass (AC-6.3). If it still misses 30 after step 3, the feature does not go online.
4. Transfer (NFR-2, AC-6.5). DevTools Network, Disable cache: load the start screen, choose Try-out, stand in the garden until no new request has come for 10 seconds; the transferred figure is at most 200 MB.
5. Loading (NFR-3, AC-6.6 to AC-6.8). Slow 4G, cache disabled: within 30 seconds the indicator is gone and our plot and the street show; plain blocks stand where the neighbourhood has not arrived and are replaced as files arrive. Then no throttling, empty cache, connection speed noted: the neighbourhood is shown within 60 seconds of the place being ready.
6. Failure drills (AC-6.15 to AC-6.18, AC-4.7). Block one car, one house, the lamp, the backdrop picture, then everything under `models/houses/`, `models/street/` and `textures/backdrop.webp`: stand-ins and the horizon band show, one message only, the place loads, our fence and gate still stop you. Then block `paving.jpg` and `asphalt.jpg` (plain colours).
7. Shadows (AC-5.5, AC-5.6). Compare the before views of Task 0 with the same views now: ours are not softer. Check that the other plots' objects cast shadows near you and may fade farther away.
8. Privacy (AC-7.1, AC-7.2). Network tab open for 5 minutes along the fence: only GETs of files, nothing about you. Compare Local Storage before and after a visit through Try-out: no new entry (A-Frame's own `debug` key is the known exception).
9. Browsers (NFR-5, AC-6.19). Latest stable Chrome, Firefox and Safari on desktop: load, walk, look, nothing blank. Frame rate is judged only in Chrome. Record versions and OS.
10. Street ends and horizon (AC-1.9, AC-4.2, AC-4.3). At each end of the front fence look along the street with a wide window: no end of the road shows, no seam in the picture, no void.
11. Go through the checklist of Task 13 row by row and write yes or no. NFR-6 (no accessibility level) is a stated limitation and has no row.
