# A Neighbourhood around the Plot: Implementation Plan

> **For Claude:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans (or superpowers:subagent-driven-development in the same session) to implement this plan task-by-task.

**Goal:** Put 7 more plots (one on each side of ours, 5 across the road) around our garden, each with a fence, a closed gate, a tiled path, a Dutch or Belgian style house and a garden, plus 8 parked cars, 8 street lamps and a static picture of buildings around the horizon. All of it is scenery, loaded after the place is ready.

**Architecture:** Plots are data (`src/world/plots.js`) built from the same pure helpers as our plot (`fenceLine`, `slabGrid`) and the same builders (fence, gate, slabs, trees, bushes, hedge, bench, table, flower bed). The plots are never added to `SOLIDS`, so collision and the 444.5 m² area stay as they are. Houses are generated by a script into four small GLBs (own work, no download), the picture is a seamless alpha band on a cylinder. Cars, lamps, houses and the backdrop load after the place is ready through the existing late-loading helper, with stand-ins until they arrive. The sun's shadow box follows the person instead of covering the whole scene, so our plot's shadows stay as sharp as today. The frame-rate steps are build-time constants.

**Tech Stack:** A-Frame 1.6 / three.js 0.170, Vite 8, Vitest (jsdom), Prettier (single quotes), ESLint 10, sharp (already a dev dependency). Vanilla ES modules. Spec: `specs/neighbourhood/spec.md` (v1). Plans live in `docs/plans/`, checklists in `docs/verification/`.

**Paths:** The app lives in `app/`. Run every command from `app/`; every `src/`, `tests/`, `public/`, `scripts/` path below is relative to `app/`. `specs/` and `docs/` are at the repository root. Read `CLAUDE.md` first; it describes the street feature this plan builds on (`specs/garden-fences-and-street/`, `docs/plans/2026-11-09-garden-fences-and-street.md`).

## Decisions for the three technical questions

**OQ-1: how the houses are built (A-4).** A script (`scripts/build-neighbour-houses.mjs`, same approach as `scripts/build-house.mjs`) generates four GLBs from data in `src/world/houseLooks.js`: one file per shape, each 8 by 5 m, with named materials (`Brick` or `Render`, `Roof`, `Window`, `Door`, `Trim`). The four shapes are:
- `brick-pitched`: 2 floors, brick, pitched tiled roof with the ridge along the 8 m side.
- `brick-stepped`: 3 floors, brick, stepped gable toward the street (Dutch), ridge along the 5 m side.
- `render-pitched`: 2 floors, rendered facade, pitched tiled roof.
- `render-stepped`: 3 floors, rendered facade, stepped gable.

The builder tints the facade material per plot (brick tints, render colours), so 7 plots show at least 4 distinct looks (a different hue or material, roof shape or floor count, as the spec's Definitions say). Each house has at least 4 windows on the street face (the facade has 2 per floor on a 2-floor house at least, so 4 or more), a front door on the street side, and dark opaque glass, so no interior shows. The files are tiny (tens of KB), made for this product (own work, recorded as CC0 dedication), and each is shared by the plots that use its shape. Downloaded house models were rejected: the Downtown City kit on disk is American and no free Dutch or Belgian set has been found. If the generated houses look poor, the builder may swap files later; the code only needs a GLB with the material names above.

**OQ-2: the backdrop picture (FR-9).** A real picture, made from free licensed photographs (CC0 or CC-BY only, no generated images), cut into one seamless horizontal strip (alpha WebP, about 4096 by 512 px) by `scripts/make-backdrop.mjs`: the sky part is made transparent, the top edge fades out, and the left and right ends are cross-faded so the strip repeats without a seam. It is drawn on the inside of an open cylinder of radius 100 m around the origin, 20 m high, standing in the ground (the far plots' corners are at about 74 m, so everything stands inside it). The material takes the fog, so it fades at that distance (fog starts at 40 m, ends at 220 m). Until the picture has arrived, or if it failed, a plain band of the horizon colour (`skyParams(assets).horizon`) of the same size stands there. The sky sphere and the grass ground (600 m) already continue behind it, so no void shows. The picture is a download task and needs the user's approval.

**OQ-3: how far the shadows reach (FR-11, FR-12).** Covering the whole scene (plots to x = ±60, z = 44, houses up to about 10 m) would need a sphere of about 76 m and would make our shadows about 3 times softer than today (7.4 cm instead of 2.66 cm per texel at 2048). Instead the shadow box becomes a sphere of fixed radius `SHADOW_RADIUS = 27` m that follows the person, centred on the person and snapped to whole texels in light space so it does not shimmer. 2 × 27 / 2048 = 2.64 cm per texel, no softer than today's 2.66 cm. Objects farther than 27 m from the person show no shadow; FR-12 allows "softer or absent" for the neighbourhood. A pure function `followSun` (`src/logic/shadowFollow.js`) computes the snapped target and the sun position; a component moves the light each frame. `shadowFrustum` takes a radius, and `SHADOW_REGION` and `SHADOW_MAX_HEIGHT` are no longer used for the box.

**Other decisions the spec left to the plan (not behaviour; all reversible):**
- **Plot frames.** Our side: plots at x −36..−12 and 12..36, z −11..11, street side at z = 11. Far side: five plots at x −60..60 (24 m each), z 22..44, street side at z = 22 (the outer face of the far sidewalk). A far plot's house faces −z.
- **Shared fences.** Our left and right fences (x = ±12) are the shared lines with the neighbours; the neighbours build only their other three edges. On the far side the boundary between two plots belongs to the plot that stays after step 3: plots 1 and 3 own the boundaries next to them (0|1 and 1|2 belong to plot 1, 2|3 and 3|4 to plot 3), plot 2 owns no side edge, plots 0 and 4 own their outer edge. Removing plots 0 and 4 therefore leaves the row of three fenced.
- **Fences and paths are scenery.** They are built from data, never pushed to `SOLIDS`; `outsideSolids()` and `walkableArea` do not change (FR-11, AC-5.2).
- **Gate and door in line.** On each plot the gate x equals the door x; the house centre is `gateU − look.doorOffset`. Gate x values are all different, at least 1.8 m from the plot corners (1.2 m clear plus half the opening), and the house stays at least 1 m from the side and back fences and at least 3 m behind the street-side fence.
- **Gate line** (the strip 1.2 m wide from the door through the gate and across the sidewalk to the curb): no lamp, tree, bush, hedge, bench, table or flower bed stands on it (tests check it).
- **Cars.** The three existing car models are reused. A car is parked parallel to the curb, centred on its plot's width. The existing cars face +x; our side keeps that and the far side is turned by π, so the two sides face opposite ways (the driving direction of each side, right-hand traffic; the builder checks the direction by eye and may swap both rotations). The body colour is set per car by tinting the body material (Task 7 reads the materials first). At least 3 colours, and no two cars of neighbouring plots on one side share one.
- **Lamps.** One per plot, on its sidewalk, the far ones turned by π so the arm reaches over the road, at least 1.2 m from the gate line.
- **Frame-rate steps (FR-13)** are constants in `src/world/neighbourhoodConfig.js`: `NEIGHBOUR_GARDENS` and `NEIGHBOUR_CARS` ('on' or 'off', step 1 turns both off), `NEIGHBOUR_SHADOWS` ('on' or 'off', step 2), `FAR_PLOT_COUNT` (5 or 3, step 3, keeps plots 1, 2, 3). Nothing switches at run time. The street feature's `STREET_CARS` and `STREET_LAMPS` go away (cars and lamps now come from the plots); `STREET_SHADOWS` stays for the street itself.
- **One load message per visit (FR-16).** A pure `onceNotice` in `assetLoader.js` lets the first load message through; the initial `warning` of `mountWorld` counts as shown.
- **Budget.** `tests/budget.test.js` goes from 90 MB to 190 MB of shipped assets (NFR-2: 200 MB first-visit transfer including page and scripts). The real size stays far below it (about 17 MB).

```js
// Pure: where the sun's shadow box stands for a person. The box is a sphere of fixed radius around the
// person. Its centre is snapped to whole texels in the light's own axes, so shadows do not shimmer
// when the person walks or the sky turns. No three, no DOM.
export function shadowFollow({ person, dir, distance, texel }) {
  const len = Math.hypot(...dir);
  const d = dir.map((v) => v / len);
  // right = up x d (normalised), up = d x right
  let right = [d[2], 0, -d[0]];
  const rl = Math.hypot(...right) || 1;
  right = right.map((v) => v / rl);
  const up = [
    d[1] * right[2] - d[2] * right[1],
    d[2] * right[0] - d[0] * right[2],
    d[0] * right[1] - d[1] * right[0],
  ];
  const p = [person.x, 0, person.z];
  const dot = (a, b) => a[0] * b[0] + a[1] * b[1] + a[2] * b[2];
  const u = Math.round(dot(p, right) / texel) * texel;
  const v = Math.round(dot(p, up) / texel) * texel;
  const along = dot(p, d); // keep the depth component of the person unchanged
  const target = [0, 1, 2].map((i) => right[i] * u + up[i] * v + d[i] * along);
  return {
    target: { x: target[0], y: target[1], z: target[2] },
    position: {
      x: target[0] + d[0] * distance,
      y: target[1] + d[1] * distance,
      z: target[2] + d[2] * distance,
    },
  };
}
```

Then no throttling, empty cache, connection speed noted: the neighbourhood is shown within 60 seconds of the place being ready.

**Step 6: Failure drills (AC-6.15 to AC-6.18, AC-4.7).** Block one car, one house, the lamp, the backdrop picture, then everything under `models/houses/`, `models/street/` and `textures/backdrop.webp`: stand-ins and the horizon band show, one message only, the place loads, our fence and gate still stop you. **Step 7: Shadows (AC-5.5, AC-5.6).** Compare the before views of Task 0 with the same views now: ours are not softer. Check that the other plots' objects cast shadows near you and may fade farther away.

**Step 8: Privacy (AC-7.1, AC-7.2).** Network tab open for 5 minutes along the fence: only GETs of files, nothing about you. Compare Local Storage before and after a visit through Try-out: no new entry (A-Frame's own `debug` key is the known exception).

**Step 9: Browsers (NFR-5, AC-6.19).** Latest stable Chrome, Firefox and Safari on desktop: load, walk, look, nothing blank. Frame rate is judged only in Chrome. Record versions and OS.

**Step 10: Street ends and horizon (AC-1.9, AC-4.2, AC-4.3).** At each end of the front fence look along the street with a wide window: no end of the road shows, no seam in the picture, no void.

**Step 11: Go through the checklist** of Task 13 row by row and write yes or no. NFR-6 (no accessibility level) is a stated limitation and has no row.
